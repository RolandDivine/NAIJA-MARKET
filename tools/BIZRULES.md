# Owner rules per business (binding, on top of tools/PLUGIN.md)

- bank (PadiTrust Bank): fenced off, NO special cheap farm loan (no 9% farm loan). Lends to the player's own businesses only on the same terms as outsiders: max 5% of bank equity per firm, 10% total, 120% collateral (plan3/group.json bankInsideGroup). Licence capital stays LOCKED in reserve (shown in HQ, never spendable). Policy, deposit and loan rates and FX move week to week. Use capital tiers, cash reserve ratio, liquidity ratio, deposit insurance, NPL rules from research. Map: POS umbrella, banking hall, vault, ATM gallery, head office. Note: other players can deposit into a player's bank (src/deals.js handles that; do not touch it).
- ibank (Kolanut Crown Capital): listings, bonds, mergers, advisory fees; SEC capital rules, fee % from research. No promised returns. No insider dealing, manipulation or fraud. Map: trading desk, deal room, boardroom, listing bell.
- trading (Blue Ticker Trading House): game money, NO promised returns, no real trading advice. Invented tickers only. Prices random-walk weekly with research volatility; settlement T+1. Brokerage commission, SEC/NGX fees, licence capital, margin rules. plan/trading.json already exists from an earlier run: reuse/fix it. Map: bureau de change counter, trading floor with ticker boards, research room.
- estate (Oke-Ile Homes & Towers): agency fees, rents, land and C of O costs, build cost per m², service charge, occupancy, mortgage rates. No fake titles, double sales or land grabbing. Map: plots, building sites with cranes, show houses, towers.
- factory (Omi-Ola Industries): sachet water to consumer goods; NAFDAC registration, power/diesel, packaging, distributor margins, machine capacity. No fake NAFDAC numbers. Map: borehole and tanks, production hall, warehouse, loading docks.
- health (Dawnfield Health Group): medical people play this. Real generic drug names, real common Nigerian illnesses, real standard ADULT doses from WHO or Nigerian national guidelines, each verified with WebSearch/WebFetch; sources listed in code comments and in plan/health.json "clinicalSources". Children: say "dose by weight". Show doses as information plus one line "For game learning only, not medical advice." Emergencies are NEVER turned away (treated even with no money; cost absorbed or claimed per research). Consultation fees, drug margins, lab tests, bed costs, doctor/nurse salaries, NHIA rules. No fake drugs. Map: chemist shop, clinic rooms, lab, wards, ambulance bay.
- hotel (Adunni Palms Hotels): room rates, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, F&B share, housekeeping ratios, power costs, VAT and consumption tax. Map: rooms block, pool, restaurant, conference hall, rooftop.
- media (Koko Reel Entertainment): wedding video, studio rates, streaming/box-office shares, royalties, distribution fees; box office and royalty rules do NOT take a quality score. Invented names only, no real artists/songs/lyrics, no piracy. Map: wedding hall, recording studio, film set, cinema.
- tech (KoboWave Technologies): phone repair to payments software; repair prices, dev rates, transaction fees, CBN payment licence tiers (incl. the ₦100M-type licence stage), server costs, churn. No fraud/hacking/data misuse. plan/tech.json already exists from an earlier run: reuse/fix it. Map: repair kiosk, dev office, server room, payments HQ.
- airline (KiteRise Air & Travels): ticket commission (research says 1%), aircraft lease about $240k a month, Jet A1 price, load factor, NCAA AOC, maintenance checks. Fuel and dollar move weekly. Safety never cut. Map: travel desk, airport apron with planes, hangar, terminal.
- energy (Kpakpando Energy): LPG per kg, generator rental, solar mini-grid cost per kW, NERC tariff bands, petrol/diesel, capacity factors. Prices weekly. No illegal fuel or pipeline tapping. Map: gas kiosk, solar farm, substation, power plant.
- council (Okele Council Services): `BIZX.council.locked()` returns "Reach level 50 in another business to serve in local government" unless levelOf(k) >= 50 for some other key in SAVE.biz. Starts in a local government office doing real LG tasks (sanitation, markets and levies, birth registration, primary health centres, roads and drains, primary schools); revenue = statutory allocation share + internally generated revenue per research (key percentOfBudget); customers are residents with requests. Branches = local government areas across several states; final venues = governorship across states. NO bribery, vote buying or rigging at all, not even as an offer; elections won only by service and trust. Neutral: invented places, no real parties or politicians. Map: council office, market, health centre, roads and drains, town hall.
