Naija Market

Pick a business and grow it from a roadside spot into an empire. Each business keeps its own progress.

Drag anywhere to walk. Walk into the farm, make a circle with your rope, and come back out to collect everything inside. Carry goods on your head, sell, and stand on the price pads to upgrade. At 8 PM, go home, eat, bath and sleep. Salaries and tax are paid every 30 days. Open HQ to grow your empire.
